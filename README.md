## 🕹️ About

Shroom and Gloom is a first-person roguelike double-deckbuilder developed by Team Lazerbeam and published by Devolver Digital. Explore hand-drawn fungal dungeons, battle monstrous mushrooms with hundreds of cards, and grow your decks as you push toward the heart of The Gloom. The game is planned to launch in Early Access on September 10, 2026.

Shroom and Gloom is for players who enjoy inventive deckbuilders, turn-based roguelikes, surreal atmosphere, and high-risk runs built around creative combos.

## ✨ Features

- 🃏 **Double Deckbuilding** — Build separate decks for combat and exploration.
- 🍄 **Fungal Foes** — Fight strange mushroom enemies across dangerous underground tunnels.
- 🌱 **Growing Cards** — Evolve cards into powerful abominations and create massive combos.
- 🔥 **Edible Enemies** — Season, skewer, roast, and eat defeated enemies to restore health.
- 🍲 **Combat Cooking** — Throw roasted foes into a pot and create game-changing soups.
- 🗺️ **Roguelike Runs** — Make meaningful choices across branching paths and repeatable runs.
- 🎨 **Hand-Drawn Dungeons** — Descend through a surreal world brought to life with distinctive hand-drawn art.
- 👥 **Playable Characters** — Choose from multiple characters with distinct card pools and playstyles.
